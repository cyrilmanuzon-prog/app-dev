# Title: Iron Man

---

##  Overview
**Iron Man**, also known as **Tony Stark**, is a genius billionaire inventor and a founding member of the **Avengers** in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).  
He first appeared in **Iron Man (2008)**, directed by Jon Favreau, played by **Robert Downey Jr.**

---

##  Why Iron Man Is Iconic
- The **first hero** of the MCU  
- Amazing character development  
- High-tech suits and futuristic weapons  
- Smart, witty, and charismatic  
- Sacrificed himself to save the universe  

---

##  Tony Stark’s Skills
- Genius-level intelligence  
- Engineering and robotics expert  
- Master inventor  
- Skilled strategist and leader
